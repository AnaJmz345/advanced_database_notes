## What I understood
- In this session I understood that creating dashboards is not only about writing SQL queries and showing numbers. Before creating a KPI, I need to define what the metric means, what business question it answers, what filters it uses, and what edge cases can affect the result. For example, a metric like team velocity can be misleading if I only count completed tasks without considering the team size or the time period. I also learned that dashboards are more useful when the data is organized by priority, status, team, or severity, because this makes the information easier to understand and compare.

## What is still confusing
- I am still a little confused about how to choose the best definition for a KPI when there are different possible interpretations. For example, team velocity could mean completed tasks per team, completed tasks per user, or completed tasks per day.
