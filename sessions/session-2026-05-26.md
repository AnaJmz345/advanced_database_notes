## What I understood

What I understood is that OLTP and OLAP are both database systems, but they are designed for very different purposes.

OLTP (Online Transaction Processing) focuses on handling daily operations and real-time transactions. It is used in systems where many users are constantly inserting, updating, or deleting data. For example, banking systems, online stores, airline reservations, or ATM transactions use OLTP because they need fast response times and accurate data processing. I understood that OLTP databases usually work with current data and many small transactions happening at the same time.

OLTP systems are highly normalized because normalization helps avoid duplicated data and keeps transactions consistent. In OLTP, performance is important for individual transactions, so queries are usually short and simple, like adding a new customer, updating an order, or checking an account balance.

OLAP (Online Analytical Processing),  is more focused on analysis and decision-making instead of daily operations. OLAP systems are used to analyze large amounts of historical data and identify patterns, trends, or business insights. Companies use OLAP for reports, dashboards, business intelligence, and data analysis.

Unlike OLTP, OLAP queries are usually complex because they process huge amounts of information at once. Instead of updating records constantly, OLAP mainly reads and analyzes data. OLAP databases are often denormalized because this improves the speed of analytical queries and reporting.

Tthe difference in purpose between both systems. OLTP is optimized for operational tasks and fast transactions, while OLAP is optimized for analysis and strategic decisions. For example, an online shopping system would use OLTP to process purchases in real time, but the company could use OLAP later to analyze sales trends, customer behavior, or monthly revenue.

OLTP usually handles current and detailed data, while OLAP works more with historical and aggregated data. OLTP supports the day-to-day activities of a business, while OLAP helps managers and analysts make decisions based on data analysis.

### Etl process
OLAP systems usually receive their data through an ETL process. ETL means Extract, Transform, and Load. First, data is extracted from different sources, for example SQL databases, CSV files, or other systems. Then, the data is transformed, which means it is cleaned, organized, and sometimes aggregated so it can be useful for analysis. Finally, the data is loaded into tables, usually inside a data warehouse or analytical database. This process is important because OLAP needs clean and structured data to create reports, dashboards, and business insights.
